About

My work sits where law, technology and operations meet. In the Global Data Privacy Office at Publicis Groupe I help run GDPR compliance projects with international reach: reviewing data processing agreements, standard contractual clauses and impact assessments, supporting data subject access requests, and finding the bottlenecks and automation gaps that slow privacy work down. Alongside this I'm studying for an M.Sc. in Management and Technology at the Technical University of Munich.

I came to privacy from the systems side. For nearly three years I implemented and supported ERP software, first Quantum Control for aviation manufacturers at Component Control, then ComputerEase at Deltek, taking clients from onboarding to rollout and tracing problems down to the SQL and Oracle back end. Before that I ran month-end and year-end close for two joint ventures at INTO University Partnerships, and played professional tennis on the ITF tour, two years full time and six months part time, earning an ATP doubles ranking.

At TUM I research innovation, energy and finance. You can find my work so far under Writing.

What I work on

  • Data privacy and GDPR compliance Data processing agreements, standard contractual clauses, DPIAs, vendor due diligence and data subject access requests.
  • ERP implementation Taking clients from onboarding to rollout, training users, and troubleshooting down to the database.
  • Process improvement Finding bottlenecks, automation gaps and documentation inconsistencies in compliance and operations workflows.
