About

I work on data privacy and legal project management in the Global Data Privacy Office at Publicis Groupe, and I'm studying for an M.Sc. in Management and Technology at the Technical University of Munich.

Before that I spent several years putting ERP systems into companies: at Component Control (Quantum Control, for aviation manufacturers) and at Deltek (ComputerEase). Earlier I worked in finance and accounting at INTO University Partnerships, and I spent a year on the ITF tour as a professional tennis player, earning an ATP doubles ranking.

What I work on

  • Data privacy and GDPR compliance Data processing agreements, standard contractual clauses, DPIAs, vendor due diligence and data subject access requests.
  • ERP implementation Taking clients from onboarding to rollout, training users, and troubleshooting down to the database.
  • Process improvement Finding bottlenecks, automation gaps and documentation inconsistencies in compliance and operations workflows.
